# Apple-Style Glass Design for Choir LMS Mobile

Use glass sparingly and only where the surface behaves like system chrome.

## Strong Targets

- The bottom dock that holds the mini-player and tab bar.
- Compact grouped playback controls that float above content.
- The frame of the playback sheet or modal sheet header.
- Small accessory controls that sit on top of a dark hero or image background.

## Weak Targets

- Discovery cards with dense text.
- Saved track rows, playlist rows, and loop editor forms.
- Long lists where translucency would reduce scanning speed.
- Full-height sheets containing dense rehearsal metadata.

## Composition Rules

- Use one or two shared glass surfaces per screen, not many isolated ones.
- Let the glass sit over meaningful color or tonal variation.
- Keep content cards inside the glass frame mostly opaque.
- Use subtle separators before adding extra shadows.
- Keep tint light. Heavy brand tint makes the surface look painted, not refractive.

## Motion Rules

- Glass works best with short, stable transitions.
- Avoid bouncing or elastic animation on every surface.
- If the sheet already slides, do not add extra blur or opacity choreography unless it improves clarity.

## Accessibility Rules

- Text and icon contrast must stay readable over both dark and light backdrops.
- Press states must remain visible even when the parent is translucent.
- Reduce Transparency must fall back to a solid or nearly solid surface.
- Do not rely on blur alone to separate surfaces. Keep borders or tonal separation when needed.
