In the landscape layout, opening Pitch or Recording from the full playback view floats full-width strips at the bottom, and the two-column sheet is squeezed above them (the piano already goes full screen there). Four options, drawn at 844 × 390 with a Dynamic Island inset on each side. Suggested: B.

A. Today the bug

B. A floating card over the right column suggested

One small card in the lower trailing corner, over the tab panel (Lyrics, Queue and the rest are tall lists that scroll under it). It holds the pitch readout and the Record control; each tool's row appears only while that tool is on. The left column, with the transport and the speed and pitch tiles, is untouched, so the sheet keeps its full height.

C. A tab in the right column alternative

D. Full screen, like the piano alternative

Why B

The playback view is a reading and listening surface: lyrics, queue and the transport matter while you sing. A card keeps all of it in view and costs one corner of the right panel, which scrolls. The piano needs the whole width, so it stays full screen there; Pitch and Recording are small enough for a card. B also gives Recording its one safety rule (the pill stays visible while a take runs).
