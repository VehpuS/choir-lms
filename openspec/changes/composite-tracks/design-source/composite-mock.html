Composite tracks

Play several isolated stems together as one library track, each with its own volume, mute and solo. Tap through the phone below; every screen is also laid out underneath. The wireframe is interactive only where it helps a decision: the mixer, presets and the creation flow.

  • A library item. It has its own row with a Composite badge and a stem count, and appears in Recents, search, playlists and the queue. The Library gets a Composites filter.
  • Made from tracks. Select tracks in the Library; Composite is one bulk action among the others and is only highlighted when the tracks are the same length. Each stem gets a name, guessed from the track name and editable.
  • Mix on the playback screen. A collapsible Mix panel: presets, then a row per stem with mute, solo and a 0 to 150% volume. Tweaks are a session mix until saved as a preset; the waveform follows the mix.
  • Loads, then plays. One loading state shows n of N stems ready. A big composite is prepared once, takes longer the first time, and opens quickly after. Memory is only mentioned when it is a problem.
  • Lyrics and sheet music come from the stems. Sheet music is combined from every stem with duplicates removed; lyrics have a picker when more than one stem has them.
