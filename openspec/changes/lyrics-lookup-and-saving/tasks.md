Pitch and onset match was 100% for every variant. Duration match was 97 to 99% for per-voice tracks once short gaps were snapped (69% without the snap), and 71 to 76% for the merged piano track, where overlapping voices defeat the per-track snap. Rendered through OSMD, the humanized SATB file reads as clean notation with correct key signature.
