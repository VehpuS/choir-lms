How should a track open its sheet music?

Two ways to reach the full-screen viewer from an item menu. Pick a track state and an option, then tap through the phone. Nothing here is saved, and the real app uses its menu icons and the same Nocturne look.

Path for this choice

A · One Sheet music action, always a manager sheet

The menu entry never changes. It opens a sheet that lists the sources with View, rename, and remove, and holds Add sheet music.

B · View sheet music when one exists

With one source the entry opens the viewer directly. With several it opens a short picker. A second entry, Manage sheet music, handles add, rename, and remove. With none it reads Sheet music and goes to Add.
