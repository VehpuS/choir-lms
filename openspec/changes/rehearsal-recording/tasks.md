- [ ] 1.2 Latency sources: read `outputLatency`, `baseLatency`, and `getOutputTimestamp` on Chrome, Safari, and Firefox with speakers, wired headphones, and Bluetooth; compare to a loopback measurement. - [ ] 1.3 Overlay sync and start-up: schedule an `AudioBufferSourceNode` against the media element, run play, pause, seek, and loop wrap for a few minutes with the track at 1×, 0.7× and 1.3×, and measure drift and clicks. Also measure the time from pressing play to the first overlay sound with the track cached and with it evicted (design Decision 13), to confirm no overlay cache is needed.
- [ ] 1.4 Mix render: in the browser, render a ten-minute recording over a track at 0.7× with a pitch shift through an `OfflineAudioContext` and the SoundTouch processor; measure time and memory, and compare the result with a live capture of the same playback. ## 14. Follow-ups (to explore with the user before any implementation)

- [x] 14.1 Show the remaining recording time in the design mock and the strip (task 5.2): decided in the mock review: shown only under 30 minutes left, warning style under five, refusal under one minute, `Storage is full` stop. The mock has the states; task 5.2 builds them.
- [ ] 14.2 Recover a recording left in progress by a crash or a closed tab: the chunked store keeps its audio and a timeline checkpoint, so it could be offered on the next launch. Explore with the user whether and how to surface it.
- [ ] 14.3 Stop the take when the track ends (design Decision 8 lists `track ends` as a stop reason): the media element's `ended` event is reported as a pause, so a take now pauses at the end of the track and waits for `Stop`. Found while building 5.4; explore with the user whether the take should stop itself or wait.
- [x] 14.4 Bug found while verifying 5.5: in the browser preview a take pressed while the track was playing near 0:22 stored `trackStartMs: 0` in its timeline (so the save sheet said `Track 0:00–0:07`). Reproduce first (the preview used a faked microphone stream and a muted media element; confirm it is not caused by that), then find whether the transport position or the clock-anchor fit from 5.3 reports 0, fix it, and add a spec that starts a take mid-track. Cause: `toWallMs` trusted `getOutputTimestamp()`, which is `{0, 0}` until the capture context's output clock starts, so a take begun soon after the microphone opened was anchored on the audio clock instead of `performance.now()` and the timeline clamped to track position 0. A zero stamp is now treated as unavailable (the recorder falls back to the arrival time); specced in `pcm-source.web.spec.ts` and checked in the browser on a fresh page.
- [ ] 14.5 Save sheet follow-ups to explore with the user before changing anything: the strip still shows `Recording saved.` with `Dismiss` underneath the sheet (should the sheet replace it?), `Delete` asks for a second tap (not in the approved mock; keep or use a confirm dialog?), and a long marker span may need a two-line limit.
