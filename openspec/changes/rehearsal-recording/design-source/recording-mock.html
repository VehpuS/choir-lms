Record, overlay, adjust, and export a take

An interactive phone and a gallery of every screen the change adds. Press the state buttons or use the controls inside the phone. Nothing is recorded or saved. This is the page for task 1.5: approve or mark up what you want changed.

  • Where it lives: a third row, Recording, in the Practice tools menu. Turning it on opens the recording strip in the dock, like the pitch monitor.
  • While recording: red dot and elapsed time, the marker just passed and the next one, and the speed and pitch chip locked. Stop is always one tap.
  • After stopping: a short save sheet with the name, Keep the original with this take (on), and the size.
  • Takes list: choose one take to overlay; spans read as marker names; loop takes are tagged; a changed original is flagged. Export and delete live in each row's menu.
  • Overlay: picking a take applies its stored speed and pitch and locks those controls. A balance slider and a Sync control sit in the dock; calibration with clicks is a quiet link, never required.
  • Export: recording or mix, to the device or to Drive, with the folder and name confirmed first.
  • Punch ranges (last slice) arm a take between two markers or the loop ends.
