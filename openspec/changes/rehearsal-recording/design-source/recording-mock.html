Record, view, overlay, and manage recordings

An interactive phone and a gallery of every screen the change adds. Press the state buttons, or use the controls inside the phone (rows open, switches switch, handles drag, search types). Nothing is recorded or saved. This is the page for task 1.5: approve or mark up what you want changed.

  • Recordings are not files. They live under their track or loop. The playback screen has a Recordings button with a count, available even when the Recording tool is off. The tool only controls the strip.
  • Where they show: a track lists its own recordings and, in a Loops section, its loops' recordings. A loop lists only its own. A recording on an adjusted copy shows under the copy and under its parent, labelled with the adjustment.
  • Opening one restores its context, locked: the loop, the adjusted copy, or the recorded segment only, with its speed and pitch applied (even if never saved). Leave recording restores what you had.
  • Punch in / out: Record ▾ → Record between… opens a sheet with the waveform. Drag the In and Out handles (they snap to markers), tap marker flags, or type times; set a pre-roll; Arm. Play from the pre-roll and it starts and stops itself.
  • Storage: a recording is on the device, automatic (may be removed to free space) or kept offline. Keeping a track offline asks about its recordings, with a remembered default in Settings. The original track audio is never copied; it comes from the normal caches or Drive.
  • Library: a Recordings view to browse, search, filter, and act on all of them. Recordings can be queued and added to playlists.
